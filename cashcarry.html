CASH &
CARRY ARB

Long spot + Short perp. Capture the full funding rate, not just the differential. Delta neutral. Collect every 8h.

🟢 Delta Neutral ⚡ Full Rate Capture 🔒 Lower Liquidation Risk
How It Works

Unlike perp↔perp arb where you capture the SPREAD between two rates, cash-and-carry captures the FULL funding rate on one exchange.

Long the spot asset → short the perp → collect funding every 8h. Delta neutral: price moves cancel out because your spot gains = your perp losses (or vice versa).

No second exchange rate needed. No basis risk between two perps. Just one exchange's funding rate — all yours.

📐 Example

BTC perp paying -0.1%/8h on Binance (longs paying shorts)

→ Buy 1 BTC spot on Coinbase ($70,000)

→ Short 1 BTC perp on Binance ($70,000 notional)

→ Collect $70/8h = $210/day on $70k deployed

→ 0.3%/day · ~109% APR

Price moves cancel: BTC drops $1k → spot loses $1k, short perp gains $1k → net $0

DIFFICULTY LEVELS
Understanding the two directions
🟢 Easy

Long Spot + Short Perp

The perp funding rate is negative — longs are paying shorts. You buy the spot asset (no leverage needed) and short the perp to collect the negative rate. Straightforward on any exchange with both spot and perp markets.

Most common · No borrow needed
🟡 Hard

Short Spot + Long Perp

The perp funding rate is positive — shorts are paying longs. You short the spot asset and long the perp. Shorting spot requires margin or borrowing the asset — adds complexity and borrow cost.

Requires margin · Factor in borrow rate

HOW TO EXECUTE
Step-by-step for the EASY direction (Long Spot + Short Perp)
1

Find an elevated negative funding rate

Use the table above to find a coin where a perp exchange is paying ≥ 0.2%/8h negative rate (longs paying shorts). The higher the rate, the more you collect. Filter for EASY opportunities.

2

Buy the spot asset

Purchase the underlying asset on any exchange with spot markets — Coinbase, Kraken, or even the same exchange as your perp. Use the same notional size you plan to short on the perp. No leverage needed for the spot leg.

3

Short the perp with equal notional

Open a short position on the perp with the same dollar value as your spot purchase. 1x leverage is sufficient — you don't need to amplify. This creates your delta-neutral hedge.

4

Hold and collect funding every 8h

Funding payments are settled every 8 hours (most exchanges). As a short, you receive funding when the rate is negative. Price moves are neutralized — spot gains = perp losses. Your only P&L is the funding rate.

5

Close both legs when rate normalizes

Monitor the funding rate. When it returns to near-zero (or the net rate after fees goes negative), close the perp short first, then sell the spot. Take your accumulated funding profit and redeploy.
